Your butler is here

A Terminal window is opening now. Terminal is where you and your butler talk. It says hello and walks you through the rest, one step at a time.

If Butler was on this Mac before, it asks whether to pick up where you left off or start fresh. Nothing of yours is deleted.

To come back any time, open the Butler app. It's in the Applications folder inside your home folder, and Spotlight finds it too.

If no window opened, open the Butler app, or open Terminal and type butler, then press Return.
